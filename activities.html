Activities

01

Education

  • Organizing issue-based workshops, seminars and sensitization programmes.
  • Training Medical Officers and Para-Medical personnel under RCH programmes.
  • Managing a special school for blind, deaf and dumb girls.
  • Conducting surveys, field studies, and HIV/AIDS, nutrition and educational programmes for tribal communities.
  • Translating Trauma Counseling, Asha and SAB training modules into Oriya.
  • Promoting nutrition and educational achievement among the Chikutia Bhunjia tribal people in Nuapada District.
02

Women Empowerment

  • Participating in relief, rehabilitation and post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) programmes.
  • Operating mobile medical health services and providing health awareness support.
  • Managing SWADHAR Homes for distressed, disadvantaged and deserted widows and women.
  • Preparing consumable items such as curd, chhatua, wicks and badi to support livelihoods.
  • Providing daily OMFED milk through distribution booths and grinding wheat, chilli, turmeric and other spices.
  • Undertaking catering services and other activities that strengthen self-reliance.
