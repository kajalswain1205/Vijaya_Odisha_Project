PROJECTS

Our Projects

Location 01

Bhubaneswar

VIJAYA's headquarters and resource centre, where most training programmes are conducted by qualified and experienced staff.

Key activities

  • Training medical and paramedical staff for five continuous years.
  • Training women in vocational trades with KVIC.
  • Women leadership, awareness and female foeticide workshops.
  • Distribution of relief materials during recent floods.

Women’s guest house

Safe, affordable accommodation for economically weaker women, managed by an all-women staff at Saheed Nagar. Contact: 0674-2545766.

Location 02

Puri

The SWADHAR Home supports up to 150 distressed, deserted and deprived women and girls with accommodation, health care, counselling, training and income-generating programmes.

Special activities

  • Supported 27 dropouts to appear for matriculation; 11 passed.
  • Helped rehabilitate 193 inmates through family support, marriages and employment.
  • Provided vocational training in agarbati, paper conversion, phenyl and appliqué work.
  • Supported cataract operations that restored the eyesight of seven aged women.

Current activities

OMFED milk distribution, cereal making, temple articles, spice grinding, food preparation and paper conversion.

Location 03

Bhadrak

A special residential school for deaf, dumb and blind girls.

Special activities

  • Enabled nine disadvantaged girls to pursue higher education.
  • Supported eyesight restoration for 22 blind girls.
  • Provided hearing aids, check-ups and sign language training.
  • Imparted vocational training for self-employment.
  • Conducted sign language training with the National Training Centre for Teachers of Deaf at Janla.
Location 04

Satyabhamapur

The Women Empowerment Centre serves a cluster of nine villages, with 250 women registered for development, health, empowerment and awareness programmes.

More than 120 women have already received training in collaboration with KVIC, Government of India.
