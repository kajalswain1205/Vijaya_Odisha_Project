Our Satisfaction

Our Satisfaction

Against the backdrop of its operation over the last few years, VIJAYA has occupied a unique position in the state's health and socio-economic empowerment programmes. From building the capacity of underprivileged, unreached and marginalized communities to providing consultancy, policy advocacy, reforms and health networking, VIJAYA has emerged as a winning team. With a vision of social equity, sustainable development, common humanity and empathy towards each other, VIJAYA has marched ahead in its victorious journey against social discrimination, gender bias, ill health, illiteracy, disability and poverty.
