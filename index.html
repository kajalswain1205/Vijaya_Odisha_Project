WELCOME TO VIJAYA

Welcome to Vijaya

VIJAYA is a front ranking NGO in the state which was formally registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 on 14th October 1996 bearing Registration No- “20154/118-1996-97”. The Registered office of the organization is located at 417, Sahid Nagar, Bhubaneswar. The organization has been registered under Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act 1976 and also registered under U/S 80 G (5) & U/S 12 AA of Income Tax Act 1961. VIJAYA as stated here has very clear vision, mission, goals and objectives.

Recent Causes.

Recent Causes.

01

Shakti Sadan, Puri

SWADHAR Greh is a Government of India-sponsored women welfare scheme supported by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. It is a rehabilitation centre for women who are victims of domestic violence, family atrocities, trafficking, rape, negligence and for runaway girls. The centre provides shelter, food, clothing, health care, counselling and vocational training, while creating opportunities to learn skills and live with dignity.

02

Binapani Helen Keller Special School

Located in Langudi village in Bhadrak district, the Vijaya-Binapani Helen Keller Special School has operated since 2005 under the Deen Dayal Rehabilitation Scheme of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India. It currently supports 28 visually impaired children and 57 hearing-impaired children studying from Class 1 to Class 10.

03

Recreation

Women who have experienced stressful situations are encouraged to engage in constructive activities and move beyond painful memories. Inmates sing devotional songs, watch television programmes on religious practice, practise yoga, meet their families and visit temples and other religious places in Bhubaneswar and nearby areas.
